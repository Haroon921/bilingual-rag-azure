# Leave Policy — Al-Wadi Logistics

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes only.

## Annual leave
Employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March, or they expire.

## Sick leave
Employees are entitled to up to 30 days of sick leave per year on full pay. A medical certificate from a licensed provider is required if the absence exceeds 2 consecutive days.

## Requesting leave
Leave longer than 5 days must be requested at least 14 days in advance. The line manager decides within 3 working days.

## Parental leave
Mothers receive 12 weeks of maternity leave on full pay. Fathers receive 5 working days of paternity leave when a child is born.

## Hajj leave
Employees may take Hajj leave of up to 10 working days once, after completing two years of service.
