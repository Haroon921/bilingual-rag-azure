# Travel and Expense Policy — Al-Wadi Logistics

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes only.

## Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for any single expense above SAR 100.

## Submission and reimbursement
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days.

## Meals
The meal allowance while travelling is up to SAR 150 per day domestically and up to SAR 250 per day internationally.

## Hotels
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to SAR 800 per night domestically and up to SAR 1,200 per night internationally.

## Flights
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer with VP approval.

## Not reimbursable
Personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades chosen for personal preference are not reimbursable.
