# Remote Work Policy — Al-Wadi Logistics

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes only.

## Eligibility
Employees become eligible for remote work after 6 months of employment. Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, on days agreed with their line manager.

## Core hours
All employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 Riyadh time on remote days.

## Security requirements
Remote work requires the company VPN and multi-factor authentication on all company systems. See the IT Security Policy for the full requirements.

## Home office allowance
A one-time allowance of SAR 1,500 is available for home office equipment and is claimed through the expense process.

## Working from outside Saudi Arabia
Working remotely from outside the Kingdom requires prior written HR approval and is limited to 15 working days per calendar year.
